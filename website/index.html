ANSWERS FOR BUILDERS

Questions teams ask before they adopt.

Short answers for operators comparing agent platforms and AI workforce tools.

What is AgentForge?

AgentForge is an open-source operating layer for AI workforces connecting goals, teams, agents, procedures, channels, memory, execution, evidence, and approvals.

Is AgentForge a chatbot?

No. Chat is one surface. AgentForge manages ownership, procedures, safe context, and proof.

Can it reduce model costs?

JEv prepares bounded context packets and routes work by capability, privacy, and cost.

Does it lock me to one provider?

No. Channels, models, tools, and compute are opt-in adapters.
